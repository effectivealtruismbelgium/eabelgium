Are you looking to align your career with your values and create meaningful change in the world? Whether you’re seeking a complete career pivot or want to enhance your current impact, these three exceptional programs offer structured pathways to help you transition into high-impact work.

## 1. Centre for Effective Altruism: Career Pivot Bootcamp

**Perfect for:** Accomplished professionals (usually with 5+ years’ experience, but not required)

This free 4-day online bootcamp is designed for professionals who want to make rapid progress on their career transition. With flexible scheduling options and a focus on concrete action rather than just planning, this intensive program helps you move from consideration to application.

**Choose your format:**

- **Weekend Option:** September 20-21 & 27-28 (perfect for working professionals)
- **Weekday Intensive:** October 6-9 (ideal if you can take focused time off)

**What makes this program unique:**

- Immediate action: You’ll actually send applications and make connections during the program
- Personalized guidance on high-impact opportunities matching your specific skills
- CV feedback and iteration with expert coaches
- Network building with other impact-driven professionals
- Proven track record with alumni now working at leading impact organizations

**Four-day journey:**

1. **Scout** high-impact paths aligned with your abilities
2. **Focus** on your top directions and specific target roles
3. **Take action** with real applications and networking outreach
4. **Make it real** by addressing obstacles and setting accountability measures

[**→ Learn more and apply here by September 14**](https://www.effectivealtruism.org/courses/bootcamp)

## 2. High Impact Professionals: Impact Accelerator Program (IAP)

**Perfect for:** Mid-career and senior professionals looking to transition into high-impact work

The **Impact Accelerator Program** is a comprehensive 6-week journey designed specifically for experienced professionals ready to make their next career move count. This free program combines individual work, group coaching, and peer mentoring to help you identify concrete paths to impact.

**What you’ll experience:**

- Weekly individual work sessions (2-3 hours) with targeted exercises
- Virtual group discussions and coaching (1.5 hours weekly)
- One-on-one sessions with fellow participants and expert facilitators
- Post-program support to maintain momentum and accountability

**Key dates:**

- Application deadline: September 21
- Program runs: October 27 — December 7

[**→ Learn more and apply here**](https://www.highimpactprofessionals.org/impact-accelerator)

## 3. Altruisme Efficace France: _Ateliers Carrière à impact_ (French-speaking program)

**Perfect for:** French-speaking professionals seeking comprehensive career alignment guidance

This program offers a thorough 8-week journey with six structured sessions, designed to help you align your professional life with your values through evidence-based methodology.

**Comprehensive approach:**

- Six online sessions over eight weeks
- Each session includes 1.5 hours of guided readings and exercises plus 1.5 hours of group discussion
- Two post-workshop follow-up sessions for continued motivation
- Small cohort format (4-6 participants) with dedicated facilitator
- Completely free participation

**Six-session framework:**

1. Identify your objectives and impact goals
2. Evaluate your current options and possibilities
3. Prioritize your most promising paths
4. Test and verify your assumptions
5. Define your concrete impact plan
6. Begin executing your strategy

This program emphasizes that meaningful career change requires active participation and is designed for professionals with established work experience who can draw on real-world insights.

[**→ Learn more and apply here by September 14**](https://www.altruismeefficacefrance.org/nos-programmes/ateliers-carriere-a-impact)
