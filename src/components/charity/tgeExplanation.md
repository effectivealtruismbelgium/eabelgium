However, thanks to the [Transnational Giving Europe (TGE)](https://www.transnationalgiving.eu/) scheme,
it is possible to do cross-border donations to this charity,
while also benefiting from the tax benefits of your donation
In Belgium, this is done through the [King Baudouin Foundation (KBS/FRB)](https://kbs-frb.be/).
The downside of this approach, is that TGE takes a **5% fee** on your donation.
However, given the 45% tax deduction available for Belgian donors, the overall benefit still makes it a highly worthwhile option.

The King Baudouin Foundation will send you a tax statement each February detailing the total amount of your donation from the previous year. By default, the certificate will be sent to your residential address as it appears in the national register. **Tax deductibility for donations in Belgium is subject to some conditions** (e.g. minimum of 40€ per organisation per year), refer to [this article](https://www.fidelium.eu/en/tax-news/taking-tax-advantage-of-donations-these-are-the-conditions) to learn more. Additionally, legislation may change over time, so please verify the current rules.

For further information, please contact the King Baudouin Foundation at <tge@kbs-frb.be> or check out [this page](https://www.transnationalgiving.eu/from-belgium).
